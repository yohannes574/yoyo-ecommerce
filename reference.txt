81. INVENTORY + ORDER CONNECTION

This is very important.

Suppose:

Laptop stock = 10

Customer orders:

2 laptops

System:

Stock = 10
     ↓
Order placed
     ↓
Reserved = 2
     ↓
Available = 8

When order is completed:

Stock decreases accordingly.

If order is cancelled:

Reserved stock is released.

This prevents overselling.
